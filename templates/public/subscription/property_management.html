How it works

From your first request to the start of management, in six clear steps.

  1. The property owner submits a property management request.

  2. The owner provides the property details and required services.

  3. The team reviews the request and assesses the property.

  4. A suitable management fee is determined based on the property and service requirements.

  5. The fee and service terms are shared with the property owner.

  6. Property management begins after the terms are agreed upon.
